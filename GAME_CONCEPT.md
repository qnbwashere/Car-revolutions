# Car Revolutions — Game Concept

## High-Level Pitch
An idle car racing manager where cars continuously complete laps to earn money. Players reinvest earnings into upgrades, unlock tracks and systems, and periodically reset progress for permanent boosts. The loop is simple, satisfying, and designed for long-term progression on both mobile and web with shared cloud saves.

## Platforms & Progress Sync
Play on:
- **Mobile:** iOS and Android
- **PC:** Web browser

Progress sync is a core system:
- Account login or guest cloud save
- Automatic cloud saves
- Shared progress between mobile and browser
- Offline progress calculated and synced on reconnect

## Core Gameplay Loop
1. Cars automatically drive laps.
2. Each lap earns money.
3. Money upgrades cars and systems.
4. Upgrades increase speed and earnings.
5. Unlock new tracks and features.
6. Reset progress for permanent boosts.
7. Repeat faster each run.

The game continues to progress even while the player is away.

## Cars System
Cars replace the spinning circles from Revolution Idle.

Each car has:
- Speed
- Acceleration
- Lap earnings
- Upgrade levels
- Special bonuses (later)

Progression unlocks:
- Multiple cars running at once
- Higher-tier cars
- Cars specialized for speed or income

Cars continue racing while offline.

## Tracks
Tracks represent progression stages.

Examples:
- Beginner oval
- City circuit
- Desert raceway
- Professional speedway
- Futuristic mega track

New tracks provide:
- Bigger rewards
- Faster scaling
- New mechanics
- More impressive visuals

## Upgrades & Multipliers
Money buys upgrades that improve performance.

Upgrade types:
- **Speed upgrades:** cars finish laps faster
- **Income upgrades:** each lap pays more
- **Automation upgrades:** auto-purchase upgrades
- **Efficiency upgrades:** reduce slowdown with many cars

Multipliers stack across systems:
- Global income multipliers
- Car bonuses
- Track bonuses
- Prestige bonuses
- Event bonuses

Numbers scale massively over time.

## Prestige Reset System
At major milestones, players unlock **Season Reset**.

Reset removes:
- Cars
- Tracks
- Money

But grants permanent rewards such as:
- Faster cars forever
- Higher earnings forever
- Global bonuses

Each new run becomes much faster.

## Higher Reset Layers
Later, deeper reset layers unlock with stronger boosts:
- Season Reset
- Championship Reset
- World League Reset
- Legend Tier Reset

Each layer:
- Resets more progress
- Grants stronger permanent bonuses
- Unlocks new mechanics

## Strategy & Management
Later gameplay becomes more strategic:
- Choosing car types
- Balancing speed vs. profit
- Optimizing upgrade paths
- Timing resets for maximum gain

## Offline Progress
Cars continue racing while the game is closed.

When players return:
- Offline laps are calculated
- Money and rewards are granted
- Bonuses apply
- Offline time may be capped unless upgraded

## Challenges Mode
Optional challenge runs modify rules for extra rewards.

Examples:
- Slow cars only
- Limited upgrades
- Reduced income
- No automation

Completing challenges unlocks permanent bonuses.

## Achievements & Milestones
Players earn permanent rewards for reaching goals:
- Total laps completed
- Upgrades purchased
- Reset milestones
- Challenge completion

## Automation Systems
The game becomes largely automated over time:
- Auto-buy upgrades
- Auto-buy cars
- Auto-switch tracks
- Auto-reset timing

Players focus on optimizing systems instead of manual clicking.

## Scaling & Progress Balance
Upgrade costs rise quickly, encouraging:
- Smarter upgrade decisions
- Build optimization
- Reset strategy

Resets remain essential for progress.

## Visual Progression
The game grows more impressive visually as players advance:

- **Early:** one slow car on a simple track
- **Mid:** multiple cars, neon tracks, boost effects
- **Late:** many cars, extreme speed, futuristic visuals

Visual growth reinforces progression.

## Events & Limited Bonuses
Temporary events keep gameplay fresh:
- Double income weekends
- Special event tracks
- Unique car unlocks
- Seasonal bonuses

## Endgame Gameplay
Late-game focus:
- Optimization
- Huge multiplier stacking
- Reset strategy
- Efficient automation

## Full Game Completion Checklist
To make the game feel **fully complete**, the release scope should include:

### Cross-Platform & Account Systems
- Unified account login with optional guest cloud save
- Reliable cloud sync for mobile + web
- Conflict resolution for saves (latest, highest, or player choice)
- Offline progression that reconciles on reconnect

### Content & Progression Depth
- Multiple car tiers with distinct roles (speed vs. income)
- Multiple tracks with escalating rewards and mechanics
- At least two reset layers beyond the first prestige
- Endgame goal: extremely high multipliers and long-term optimization

### Automation & Quality of Life
- Auto-buying for cars and upgrades
- Auto-track switching logic
- Auto-reset timing with user tuning
- Clear upgrade paths and progression guidance

### UX, Feedback, and Visual Growth
- Strong visual progression from early to late game
- Clear UI feedback for laps, earnings, and boosts
- Milestone celebrations for new cars, tracks, and reset tiers
- Performance-friendly visuals for many cars on screen

### Longevity & Retention Systems
- Achievements and milestone rewards
- Limited-time events with temporary boosts
- Challenge modes with unique modifiers
- Seasonal or time-based content refreshes
